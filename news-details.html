DHAKA: In a country where non-profit transparency and long-term sustainability are often under scrutiny, Aloy Welfare Foundation has emerged as a beacon of high governance standards, direct community impact, and zero-compromise accountability. A special investigative feature published by the country's highest-circulating daily newspaper, Bangladesh Pratidin, commended the organization's swift transformation from a grassroots youth initiative into one of the nation's most trusted humanitarian foundations.

The newspaper's field reporting underscored how Aloy Foundation operates strictly on an audited, direct-to-beneficiary operational model. By pairing humanitarian aid with income-generating physical assets—such as heavy-duty sewing machines, cargo utility vans, interest-free micro capital, and modern cataract eye operations—the organization does not merely hand out temporary relief; it permanently breaks the cycle of generational poverty.

Key Focus Areas Highlighted by National Media

During the investigative survey conducted across three coastal districts—Feni, Noakhali, and Bhola—journalists observed the tangible impact of Aloy Foundation's key flagship programs:

  • Aloy Pathshala & Dropout Prevention: Supplying daily cooked hot lunches, free school bags, textbooks, and full higher secondary scholarships so children from day-laborer households do not end up in hazardous child labor.
  • Frontline Healthcare in River Islands: Conducting specialized medical camps with certified MBBS doctors, distributing free prescription medicines, and restoring eyesight for elderly villagers via modern phaco cataract surgery.
  • Women Empowerment Through Sewing Machines: Training rural widows and unemployed women in tailoring and handing over brand-new sewing machines, giving them immediate earning power with dignity.
  • Asset-Based Poverty Alleviation: Gifting pedal and battery rickshaws to struggling breadwinners, allowing them to keep 100% of their daily earnings instead of paying steep garage rents.
"ত্রাণ বা সাময়িক সহায়তা কাউকে চিরতরে দারিদ্র্যমুক্ত করতে পারে না। কিন্তু একটি রিকশা, একটি সেলাই মেশিন বা একজন শিক্ষার্থীর পড়াশোনার নিশ্চয়তা পুরো পরিবারের ভবিষ্যৎ বদলে দেয়। আলোয় ফাউন্ডেশন সেই বাস্তব কাজটিই করে দেখাচ্ছে।"
Senior Editorial Columnist — Bangladesh Pratidin (National Desk)

Recognized Governance & Full Public Disclosures

The media report also shed light on Aloy Foundation's institutional credentials. Registered under the NGO Affairs Bureau (Prime Minister's Office) with registration AF-8912 and granted statutory 100% tax exemption under Section 44 of the Income Tax Act by the National Board of Revenue (NBR), every transaction is certified by chartered accountant audits and open to public inspection.

"We believe transparency is not an option; it is our sacred duty to every donor and every helpless family we serve," said an Aloy Foundation official during an interview with the daily. "When people see their hard-earned Zakat and Sadaqah directly turning into sight for a blind elder or books for a child, trust blossoms naturally."

The news article concluded with a call to action for conscientious citizens, corporate CSR divisions, and expatriate Bangladeshis to collaborate with verified humanitarian foundations like Aloy to accelerate socio-economic progress across the country.
