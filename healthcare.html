Free Medical Camps & Mobile Clinics

MBBS Doctors & Free Diagnostics

We organize routine free medical camps in coastal islands and remote villages where government hospitals are hard to reach. Qualified specialist doctors provide patient consultations, blood tests, ultrasounds, and free full-course medicines.

Bi-weekly Camps

Free Cataract & Vision Restoration

Phaco Surgeries & Free Spectacles

Curable cataract blindness leaves countless poor village elders dependent on others. Our specialized ophthalmology teams screen rural patients, transport them to surgical centers, perform phacoemulsification with intraocular lens, and provide post-op medications.

Restoring Sight

Maternal & Child Health Care

Pre-natal, Delivery & Infant Nutrition

Combating high infant mortality and maternal malnutrition. We provide regular gynecological checkups, iron/folic acid supplements, safe delivery kits, baby hygiene essentials, and high-protein nutrition rations for pregnant mothers and toddlers.

Safe Motherhood

24/7 Ambulance & Oxygen Bank

Rapid Emergency Transit & Oxygen Cylinders

In critical emergencies, every minute matters. Our fleet of emergency ambulances provides free transport for destitute critical patients to district hospitals, while our free oxygen bank supplies oxygen concentrators and cylinders directly to homes.

24/7 Emergency Line
