Healthcare

Health Support

Bringing Compassionate Healthcare to the Underserved A large segment of Bangladesh’s population still lacks access to basic healthcare due to poverty, remote living conditions, high treatment costs, and low awareness. To change this reality, MASTUL Foundation operates its comprehensive Health Support Program, ensuring accessible and affordable medical services for marginalized communities.
