Tree Plantation & Climate Action

Building a better society through small regular gifts

নিয়মিত ক্ষুদ্র অনুদানে একটি সুন্দর আগামী গড়ার গল্প
Oct 12, 2026 Fulgazi, Feni Aloy Field Team 4 min read
Share:
Aloy Foundation volunteers and local farmers planting fruit-bearing saplings in Fulgazi, Feni.
প্রতিমাসে ছোট ছোট উপহার একত্রিত হয়ে কীভাবে গ্রামীণ পরিবেশে একটি টেকসই পরিবর্তন আনে, তার অনন্য উদাহরণ ফুলগাজী ও ফেনীর বৃক্ষরোপণ কর্মসূচি। আলোয় ফাউন্ডেশনের নিয়মিত সাধারণ দাতাদের সহায়তায় ৫,০০০ এর অধিক ফলদ ও বনজ বৃক্ষ রোপণ করা হয়েছে।

In a changing climate where recurring flash floods and severe erosion threaten marginal farmers along riverbanks, planting trees is not merely an environmental ritual—it is a vital lifeline for economic survival and soil stabilization. For millions living in remote rural pockets of Bangladesh, losing a homestead to seasonal soil degradation means facing immediate destitution.

Recognizing this urgent crisis, the Aloy Welfare Foundation launched the "Green Roots for Tomorrow" initiative, powered directly by small, recurring donations from generous people across Bangladesh and abroad. Rather than depending solely on unpredictable annual grants, this program pools micro-donations—as little as ৳100, ৳250, or ৳500 per month—to sustain a reliable, year-round tree-planting campaign.

Community-Led Sustainability in Action

Unlike conventional tree distribution drives where saplings are handed out and forgotten, Aloy Foundation pairs every sapling gift with hands-on agricultural guidance and natural pest-management training. In collaboration with local village committees and youth clubs, our field officers map out erosion-prone homestead embankments, village school playgrounds, and rural roadsides.

Special priority was given to high-yield fruit varieties such as mango, guava, amla (amla/amla emblic), jackfruit, and mahogany timber saplings. These trees not only provide shade and prevent soil erosion, but also guarantee vitamin-rich nutrition for young children and a reliable secondary source of income when fruits are harvested for the local bazaar.

"একটি গাছ শুধু ছায়াই দেয় না, আমাদের মতো নিঃস্ব পরিবারের কাছে এটি সন্তানের মতো পরম নির্ভরতা। গত বন্যার পর আমার বাড়ির সীমানা ভেঙে যাচ্ছিল। আলোয় ফাউন্ডেশন থেকে দেওয়া পেয়ারা আর আমলকীর চারাগুলো আমার উঠানের মাটি আটকে রেখেছে এবং পরিবারের ভিটামিনের অভাব মেটাচ্ছে।"
Md. Abdul Gafur, 62 — Smallholder Farmer, Fulgazi, Feni

The Power of Regular Micro-Giving

Many believe that solving colossal humanitarian challenges requires astronomical wealth. However, the success of the Fulgazi project demonstrates that consistent generosity—no matter how modest—creates compound impact over time. When 500 supporters pledge ৳200 every month, an entire union can be revitalized with tree coverage, clean water, and organic nourishment.

Looking Forward: Scaling to Coastal Belts

Building on the milestones achieved in Feni, Aloy Foundation is expanding the tree plantation and disaster resilience model to the coastal unions of Bhola and Noakhali in the coming months. With the monsoon season approaching, our field teams are preparing nursery seedbeds and mobilizing local volunteers to plant an additional 10,000 mangrove and timber saplings along coastal dikes.

We invite every caring soul to take part in this journey of spreading light and green hope. Together, one small gift at a time, we are safeguarding our land and leaving a flourishing Bangladesh for generations to come.
